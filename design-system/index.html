02 — Official logo system

The supplied lockups are the source of truth.

Do not redraw, typeset, stretch, recolor individual pieces or alter the proportions. Preserve clear space equal to at least the height of the small triangular element.

Primary color

Use on white and very light neutral surfaces.

Monochrome mark

Use for formal documents, watermark treatments and reduced-color applications.

Reversed white

Use on navy, black or sufficiently dark photographic backgrounds.

  • Minimum digital width for the full lockup: 180 px.
  • Never place the full logo directly over a busy photograph without a dark overlay.
  • The accent green belongs to the interface system; do not recolor the official logo unless a new approved master is supplied.

04 — Typography

Anton, Poppins Bold and Libertinus Math.

The typography system now uses a stronger, more distinctive hierarchy while maintaining generous line spacing and consistent alignment.

Anton / display

Clear decisions. Confident outcomes.

Use for hero headlines, section headlines and high-impact campaign statements. Keep lines short.

Poppins Bold / UI

Services built around your case.

Use for card titles, navigation, buttons, labels, outcomes and important numbers.

Libertinus Math / editorial

“Your case deserves clarity, not confusion.”

Use for body copy, detailed explanations, quotes and softer editorial moments.

Recommended scale

Display 72–124 px · H1 52–78 px · H2 40–58 px · H3 22–28 px · Body 18–21 px · UI labels 11–14 px.

Spacing rules

Headline to lead: 24–34 px · Lead to content grid: 48–56 px · Paragraph line-height: 1.7–1.8 · Buttons use 50 px minimum height.

05 — Human imagery

Show relief, confidence and real progress.

Prioritize documentary-style photographs of clients and consultants in believable moments rather than generic illustrations or legal clichés.

Approval moment

A client reading positive news with a natural, restrained reaction.

Supported process

Consultant and client reviewing documents side by side.

Confident next step

A calm client leaving a meeting with organized materials.

Do

Natural or soft office light · diverse people and family structures · real documents and devices · genuine reactions · useful negative space.

Avoid

Handshake clichés · gavels and courthouse stock imagery · exaggerated celebrations · call-center headsets · heavy blue overlays on skin.

06 — Iconography & texture

Structured symbols, not playful cartoons.

Use one outlined icon family with 1.75–2 px strokes, rounded joins and restrained geometry. Focus on documents, decisions, protection, communication and progress.

Texture principle

Use fine document grids, cropped geometry from the Prisma mark, soft green radial light, restrained glass panels and subtle paper grain. Keep texture below 12% opacity behind text.

08 — Layout, accessibility & handoff

The shared contract between design and development.

The system uses a consistent spacing scale so content, cards and actions feel calm and intentionally aligned.

Grid

12 columns desktop · 8 tablet · 4 mobile · 1280 px max content · 24 px gutters.

Spacing

8 px base unit · 88–132 px section padding · 28–34 px card padding · 26 px grid gaps.

Shape

20 px card radius · 10 px controls · 1 px borders · shadows used sparingly.

Accessibility

18 px body target · 44×44 px minimum touch targets · visible focus · WCAG AA · status never communicated by color alone.
